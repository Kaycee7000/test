# Narrator voices (Chatterbox reference clips)

Each channel YAML points `voice.chatterbox_ref` at a clip here, e.g. `assets/voices/history_en.wav`.
Chatterbox clones the timbre and delivery of this clip for every narration. **This clip is your brand.**
A distinctive narrator voice people recognise is one of the clearest signals that a channel is a real show
and not mass-produced content.

## Recording a good reference

- 10-20 seconds, a single speaker, no music, no reverb, no noise. WAV, 24 kHz or higher, mono.
- Read in the exact energy you want: documentary-intense for history, confident and wry for money, awe for space.
- Speak the channel language (the Spanish channel needs a native Spanish reference).
- Trim leading and trailing silence.

## Rights (non-negotiable)

Use **your own voice**, a voice actor who signed a release allowing AI voice cloning for your channels, or a
licensed synthetic voice. Never clone a celebrity, a famous narrator or any real person without written
consent. That violates YouTube's policies and personality rights laws, and gets channels removed.

If the file is missing, the standard Chatterbox model falls back to its built-in voice (turbo requires a clip).
